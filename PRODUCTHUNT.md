---

## Long Description

**ANKR Interact** is the open-source alternative to Obsidian + Notion + NotebookLM + Google Classroom + Duolingo + Miro — unified.

**What you get:**
- 📚 **Smart note-taking** — block editor (Tiptap), bidirectional links, knowledge graph, daily notes
- 📦 **Interactive Bundles (.ib)** — zip-based portable learning packages with docs, flashcards, quizzes, courses, AI characters, comics, and audio
- 🤖 **AI Tutor** — ask questions about your notes, get Feynman-style explanations
- 🎓 **Classrooms** — teachers create courses, assign bundles, track attendance, gamify learning
- 🌍 **23 languages** — full RTL support, Hindi voice AI, multilingual TTS
- 🏪 **Marketplace** — sell or share bundles globally (70% creator share, Razorpay)
- 📱 **Mobile-first** — Expo app with offline sync (SM-2 spaced repetition, SQLite)
- 🔒 **Self-hosted** — Apache 2.0, Docker Compose, BYOK (your own Anthropic key)
- 🆓 **Free forever** — cloud tier available at ₹299/month (Pro) or ₹999/month (Teams)

**The .ib bundle format** is our core innovation — a portable knowledge unit you can download, share, sell, or remix. Think of it as an ebook that can quiz you, teach you through a comic character, and sync to your phone.

**Built in public by ANKR Labs** — an enterprise platform company from Gurgaon, India. We eat our own dog food: all our internal training (logistics SOPs, maritime compliance, security certifications) runs on ANKR Interact.
